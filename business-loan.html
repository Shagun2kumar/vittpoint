Business Documents to Prepare

Keep relevant records ready. Exact requirements depend on the lender and product.

Identity & Registration

PAN, identity proof, business registration and authorised signatory documents, as applicable.

Bank Statements

Business bank statements for the period requested by the lender.

Financial Statements

Income tax returns, balance sheets and profit-and-loss statements where required.

GST & Sales Records

GST returns, invoices and turnover records, where applicable.

Existing Borrowings

Outstanding balances, repayment schedules and current EMI obligations.

Funding Plan

Cost estimates, quotations, cash-flow projections and intended use of funds.

This checklist is for preparation only. A lender may request additional documents or apply different eligibility criteria.
