BE PREPARED BEFORE APPLYING

Personal Loan Eligibility

Lenders assess several factors before making a lending decision. This section provides general guidance, not a guarantee of approval.

Common Eligibility Factors

  • Age within the lender's accepted range.
  • Regular and verifiable income.
  • Employment or business stability, where applicable.
  • Credit history and repayment track record.
  • Existing EMIs and other financial commitments.
  • Required identity, address and income documentation.

Exact criteria vary by lender, product and applicant. Meeting general criteria does not guarantee approval.
