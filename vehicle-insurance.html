Compare common motor insurance types before deciding which one suits your vehicle.

Third-Party Insurance

Provides cover for eligible third-party legal liabilities arising from an insured vehicle accident, subject to policy terms.

  • Third-party bodily injury liability
  • Third-party property damage, subject to limits
  • Required motor cover where mandated by law
Enquire About This Cover
Popular Option

Comprehensive Insurance

Combines third-party liability cover with own-damage protection as defined by the policy.

  • Third-party liability cover
  • Covered accidental damage to your vehicle
  • Theft and specified natural perils, subject to terms
Explore Comprehensive Cover

Standalone Own-Damage

Designed to cover specified damage to your own vehicle when paired with eligible third-party cover.

  • Covered accidental damage
  • Covered theft and specified perils
  • Availability subject to eligibility and policy rules

Extra Protection, When You Need It

Add-ons may extend your cover beyond the standard policy. Availability varies by insurer, vehicle and policy terms.

Engine Protection

Return to Invoice

May bridge the difference in eligible total-loss or theft claims.

Claims Information

Know What to Do After an Accident

Prioritise safety, contact appropriate emergency services when necessary, notify your insurer promptly and follow its claim instructions. Coverage and claim settlement depend on the policy terms and assessment.

View Claim Steps
Claim Checklist

Common Steps After a Claim Event

Always follow the specific instructions issued by your insurance company.

Ensure Safety

Move to a safe place if possible and contact emergency services if required.

Notify the Insurer

Report the incident promptly using the insurer's official claim channel.

Keep Evidence

Where safe and appropriate, record the incident details and retain relevant documents.

Submit Documents

Provide the requested documents and wait for the insurer's assessment and instructions.

Questions About Vehicle Insurance?

Find answers to common questions about vehicle cover, policy renewal, add-ons and claims.

Ask About Your Vehicle
Vehicle insurance is a policy that provides specified financial protection against covered risks and liabilities, subject to the terms, limits, deductibles and exclusions in the policy.
Third-party insurance covers specified legal liabilities to others. Comprehensive insurance generally combines third-party liability cover with own-damage protection for covered events. Check the policy wording for exact coverage and exclusions.
Premiums can depend on vehicle type, make, model, age, location, insured declared value where applicable, selected cover, add-ons, claim history and insurer pricing. The insurer provides the actual quote.
Renewal may be possible, but the insurer's process and inspection requirements can vary. Contact the insurer promptly and do not assume that cover is active until the policy has been issued and its validity confirmed.
Depending on the incident, an insurer may request policy details, registration certificate, driving licence, claim form, photographs, repair estimates, FIR or other supporting documents where applicable.
