Before You Apply

Check the Key Home Loan Eligibility Factors

Lenders assess several factors before approving a home loan. Reviewing them in advance can help you prepare for the application process.

Important: Eligibility criteria, minimum income, credit requirements and loan amounts vary by lender. Final approval is subject to the lender's assessment.
Discuss Your Requirements

Age and Applicant Profile

Lenders consider the applicant's age, residency, employment profile and applicable lending criteria.

Income and Repayment Capacity

Monthly income, regular expenses and existing EMIs help lenders assess repayment capacity.

Credit History

Your credit report and repayment history may influence the lender's decision and loan terms.

Property and Down Payment

Property valuation, legal checks, own contribution and lender-specific requirements are also relevant.

Get Prepared

Documents You May Need

The final checklist depends on your lender, applicant category and property transaction.

Identity & Address

  • PAN card, as applicable
  • Aadhaar or other accepted ID
  • Address proof
  • Photograph and application form

Income Documents

  • Recent salary slips, if salaried
  • Bank statements
  • ITR and financial records, if required
  • Employment or business proof

Property Documents

  • Agreement or sale deed documents
  • Property title documents
  • Approved plans, where applicable
  • Additional lender-requested records

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful things to know before starting your home loan enquiry.

What is a home loan?
A home loan is financing provided by a lender for an eligible residential property purchase or other permitted housing purpose. The borrower repays it according to the agreed loan terms.
Who can apply for a home loan?
Eligible salaried individuals and self-employed applicants may apply, subject to the lender's age, income, credit, property and other requirements.
What determines the home loan interest rate?
Rates depend on factors such as the lender, loan product, credit profile, loan terms and prevailing lending policies. Confirm the applicable rate and charges directly with the lender before proceeding.
How much home loan can I get?
The amount depends on your repayment capacity, income, existing obligations, property value, own contribution and the lender's eligibility assessment.
Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?
No. An enquiry is only an initial step. Loan approval, interest rates and disbursement are subject to the lender's verification and final decision.
